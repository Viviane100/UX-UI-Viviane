## 💡 My Approach

I approach digital design through three complementary perspectives:

**Understand → Design → Build**

1. **Understand:** Identify the problem, the users, their needs, and the business context.
2. **Design:** Structure the experience, explore solutions, and create purposeful interfaces.
3. **Build:** Understand the technical requirements and progressively turn design decisions into functional experiences.

My goal is to develop the ability to move confidently between these perspectives.
